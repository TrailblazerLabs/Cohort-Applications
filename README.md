# 🚀 Trailblazer Labs: Cohort Applications

Welcome to the official pitch board for the **Trailblazer Labs Builder in Residence** program — a community-driven initiative designed to surface, validate, and scale the best community-built assets within the Salesforce ecosystem.

## 🗳️ How to Engage

We use GitHub Discussions to manage all applications and community voting.

### 💡 1. Pitch an Idea
Anyone in the community can submit a contribution or idea they are willing to build and share.
1. Navigate to the **Discussions** tab.
2. Click **New Discussion** and select the **Pitch an Idea** category.
3. Fill out the automated Cohort Application form:
   - **Elevator Pitch** — what you're building, in 2–3 sentences.
   - **Problem Statement** — the gap or pain point it solves.
   - **Development Track** — Strategist, Declarative Developer, or Engineer.
   - **Builder Profile** — a bit about you and your Salesforce background.
   - **🔗 Trailblazer Community Profile** — link your [trailblazer.me](https://trailblazer.me) profile so reviewers can see your badges, credentials, and community history.

### ⬆️ 2. Upvote What We Build Next
The broader community upvotes the submissions they find most valuable.
1. Browse the pitches in the **Discussions** tab.
2. Use the upvote arrow to vote for the assets you want to see accepted into the next cohort.

## 🛠️ The Tracks

We believe everyone is a builder, and this program is fully inclusive of all skill levels across Salesforce technologies. Whether you're designing visionary frameworks and business processes, or deploying complex applications to showcase the "art of the possible," there's a place for your innovation here. All pitches should align with one of our three core tracks:

* **🧭 The Strategist (No-Code):** Decision-making frameworks, Agentforce prompt libraries, governance models.
* **⚡ The Declarative Developer (Low-Code):** Complex Flows, AI Agent configurations, App Builder templates.
* **⚙️ The Engineer (Pro-Code):** Apex triggers, LWCs, external API integrations, CLI plugins.

## 📋 Selection Criteria & Expectations

The final cohort is selected using a two-tier evaluation: **community upvotes** to prove demand, and an **internal grading rubric** to ensure feasibility, security, and quality.

If selected, builders are given a strict deadline to have their assets built and documented *before* the cohort officially launches. During the subsequent **180-day residency**, builders act as the lead maintainer and evangelist for their contribution before eventually handing it off to the community.
